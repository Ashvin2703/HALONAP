Refund & Returns

Last updated: 21 November 2026

We want you to love your HALONAP sleep mask. If it is not right for you, here is how returns work. Your legal rights are not affected.

30-day returns for unused items

You can return an unused mask, in its original packaging, within 30 days of delivery for a refund. Because sleep masks are worn on the face, we cannot accept change-of-mind returns of masks that have been taken out of their packaging or used.

Faulty, damaged or wrong item

If your mask is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered, we will put it right and pay the return postage, whether or not it has been opened. Email us with your order details and a photo. See section 7 of our Terms for your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

Your 14-day right to cancel

You also have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of receiving your order, as explained in section 6 of our Terms. This does not apply to masks sealed for hygiene reasons that have been unsealed after delivery.

How to return

  1. Tell us. Email hello@halonap.com with your order number and reason, or use the form below.
  2. Pack it. Put the unused mask back in its original packaging and include your order number.
  3. Send it to: HALONAP Returns, 81 Henry Street, Liverpool, L1 5BU, United Kingdom
    Use a tracked service and keep your proof of postage. For change-of-mind returns, you pay the return postage.
  4. Refund. We refund to your original payment method within 14 days of receiving the item (or your proof of postage). Refunds include the standard delivery charge you paid, unless you are returning only part of a multi-item order.
