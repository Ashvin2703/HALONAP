A few helpful details

Good to know.

Can I adjust the strap?

Yes. The turquoise strap has an adjustable buckle. Move it to find a comfortable fit, without making it too tight.

What colour is the mask?

The mask shown here has a black front, white halonap branding, and a bright turquoise strap with a black buckle.

How do I care for my mask?

Hand wash only in cold water (maximum 30°C). Air dry flat. Do not iron or tumble dry.

What is the mask made with?

Breathable, multi-layered memory foam with a silky-smooth outer shell. The contoured 3D pockets are designed to keep pressure off your eyes and lashes.

Does it come with a warranty?

The product packaging states a 1-year limited warranty. Full warranty terms and registration details will be provided when ordering opens.

Any packaging safety advice?

To avoid danger of suffocation, keep the packaging away from babies and children.
