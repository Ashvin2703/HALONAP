UK Standard Delivery costs £2.99 (estimated 3 business days), or is free on orders of £70 or more. UK Express Delivery costs £3.99 (estimated 1–2 business days). Times are estimates and may vary due to weekends, bank holidays, location and courier delays.

Can I return my order?

HALONAP offers a 30-day return request window from delivery for eligible items. Separately, for eligible UK distance purchases, your statutory cancellation right generally allows 14 days after delivery to notify us, followed by another 14 days to send goods back. Change-of-mind return postage may be payable by the customer where disclosed before purchase. Hygiene-seal exceptions may apply; statutory rights for faulty goods are unaffected.
