Warranty & Product Rights

HALONAP customers have legal rights if a product is faulty, damaged or not as described. These rights apply under UK consumer law and are not limited by any voluntary manufacturer warranty.

1-Year Limited Manufacturer's Warranty

Coverage: Valid for one year from the date of purchase. This limited warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship under normal use.

Not covered: Physical damage, accidental damage, misuse, or damage caused by improper care are not covered by this warranty.

To ask about warranty coverage or make a claim, contact HALONAP via WhatsApp with your proof of purchase. This warranty does not affect your statutory consumer rights.

For a problem with your sleep mask, contact HALONAP with your order details and a description of the issue. We will review the available remedies under applicable consumer law.
