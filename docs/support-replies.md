# HALONAP customer support playbook

**Targets:** reply within 24 hours · dispatch within 48 hours of payment.

**Message status in Admin Studio → Customer Messages:** `New` → set `In Progress` when you reply → `Resolved` when the customer has what they need.

Replace anything in `[brackets]`. Keep the customer's reference (`HN-…`) in the subject.

---

## Where is my order?

> Hi [name],
>
> Thanks for getting in touch. Your order [order reference] was dispatched on [date] with tracking number [tracking]. UK Standard Delivery usually arrives within 3 business days of dispatch.
>
> If it hasn't arrived by [date + 3 business days], reply here and we'll sort it out straight away.
>
> Sleep well,
> HALONAP Customer Care

## Order not dispatched yet

> Hi [name],
>
> Thanks for your patience. Your order [order reference] is being packed and will be dispatched by [date]. You'll get an email with your tracking number as soon as it leaves us.
>
> Sleep well,
> HALONAP Customer Care

## Return request (within 30 days)

> Hi [name],
>
> We're sorry the mask wasn't right for you. You can return it within 30 days of delivery. Please send it to:
>
> [return address]
>
> Include your order reference [order reference] in the parcel. Once it arrives we'll refund your original payment method within 5 business days.
>
> Sleep well,
> HALONAP Customer Care

*After the parcel arrives:* Admin Studio → Payments → **Refund remaining payment**, then add the mask back in **Products & Stock** if it can be resold.
