## Product Overview
**One-liner:** A 3D contoured sleep mask with soft eye pockets and an adjustable strap, for rest at home and on the go.
**What it does:** Shaped eye pockets leave room around the eyes and lashes. The contoured fit helps reduce unwanted light while you rest, and the adjustable strap lets each person find a comfortable fit.
**Product category:** Sleep masks / eye masks for sleeping, in the 3D contoured sub-type. On Amazon UK this is Health & Personal Care > Sleep & Snoring Aids > Sleep Masks.
**Product type:** D2C e-commerce, single product in 3 colours. Sold on halonap.com, and coming soon to Amazon UK.
**Business model:** One-off purchase. £12.99 per mask in Black, Pink or Grey. UK Standard delivery £2.99 (about 3 business days), Express £3.99 (1–2 business days), free standard delivery from £25 (2 masks). Payment by Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Amex, Onelink and Klarna.
**Materials:**
- Upper fabric, lower fabric and contact surface: 95% nylon, 5% spandex
- Upper and lower interlayers: polyurethane sponge
- Middle filling: polyurethane sponge / memory foam
**What's included:** One mask in the chosen colour, with a zip pouch.
**Unit cost:** £3.14 landed per mask (cost to have it in hand in the UK).
**Business:** HALONAP is the trading name of a UK sole trader based in Liverpool.

## Target Audience
**Target customers (B2C):** Anyone in the UK who struggles to sleep because of light or finds it hard to switch off: women and men, at home and travelling ("Made for everyone"). Search sub-groups to speak to: side sleepers, people with lash extensions, men (Amazon UK autocomplete).
**Decision-makers:** The buyer is usually the user. Gift buyers (partners, friends) are a likely second group at Christmas and Valentine's (not yet confirmed by sales).
**Primary use case:** Blocking light and switching off to sleep at home.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Help me fall asleep when the room isn't dark enough.
- Let me rest on a train, plane or overnight stay.
- Give me a calm moment to switch off during the day.
**Use cases:**
- Evening wind-down at home ("Settle into your evening routine")
- Naps on a break ("Make a little time to switch off")
- Travel and stays away ("Bring familiar comfort on your travels")
- Wearing a mask without pressing on lashes or lash extensions

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Light and distraction make it hard to switch off, at home and while travelling.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Flat masks press on the eyes and lashes and can feel tight (site comparison: "Contoured eye space — not always" on basic flat masks).
- Cheap masks (£5–8 on Amazon) look and feel generic, and are not something you'd give as a gift.
- Premium blackout masks cost £23–55 (Manta, Loop, Drowsy, Slip).
**What it costs them:** Poorer rest, tired mornings. **TBC:** no customer research yet.
**Emotional tension:** Wanting a small, nice-feeling moment of calm without overspending. **TBC:** confirm with customers.

## Competitive Landscape
*Amazon UK prices and figures as read on 2026-11-27 (Black Friday week).*
**Direct:**
- **MyHalos ("Halo Masks")**: #1 Sleep Masks bestseller, £7.99, 27,452 reviews, 10,000+ bought last month, 7 colours plus a 2-pack. Falls short because it looks generic and is not giftable. **Name risk:** "Halo" is close to HALONAP, so trademark search overlap is possible.
- **Facemoon**: £7.99, aimed at women, pink-first, weighted feel, lash-friendly. Closest to HALONAP's Pink buyer, for £5 less.
- **Gritin**: £4.99–6.29 across 7+ near-identical listings. Cheapest, with no brand feel.
- **Alaska Bear deep contoured**: £13.59, the most direct price rival in the middle tier.
**Secondary:**
- **Premium blackout masks** (Manta £23.20–55.20, Loop Eclipse £39.95, Drowsy £48.50, Slip £45). They fall short on price for an everyday or gift buyer.
- **Silk flat masks** (Alaska Bear silk, OLESILK, BeeVines). Soft, but flat and with no eye room.
**Indirect:** Blackout curtains or blinds (no help when travelling), and simply putting up with light.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "There are 3D masks on Amazon for £5–8." | Point to the design, the memory foam filling, the zip pouch and UK support, so the £12.99 is visibly justified. |
| "Will it block all light?" | Yes: 100% blackout (confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-27). Keep a record of how it was tested, since UK ad rules (ASA/CAP) require proof for absolute claims. |
| "Can I wear it lying on my side?" | Yes: side-sleeper friendly (confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-27). |
| "Will it press on my lashes or extensions?" | The 3D eye pockets create room around the eyes and eyelashes (site FAQ). |
| "Delivery adds cost." (£15.98 for one mask) | Free standard delivery from £25, so 2 masks for £25.98. On Amazon, use FBA/Prime. |
| "Can I return it?" | 30-day return request window for eligible items. Hygiene-seal exceptions may apply, and statutory rights are unaffected. |

**Anti-persona:** Shoppers who only want the cheapest mask. People who need a medical device or treatment for a sleep disorder. People who want built-in headphones or silk.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Flat masks that press on the eyes, cheap masks that slip or feel scratchy, light leaking in.
**Pull:** Contoured comfort, a nice-looking mask, colour choice, travel-ready.
**Habit:** Already owning a cheap mask that "works well enough", or not using a mask at all.
**Anxiety:** Paying more and it not fitting, it not blocking enough light, or not being able to return a worn item (hygiene).

## Brand Voice
| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean | Example line |
|-----------|-------|--------------|--------------|
| Calm | Short, quiet, unhurried sentences | Sleepy, vague or dull | "A little moment of calm." |
| Warm | Kind and personal, like a friend | Gushing, lots of exclamation marks | "Sleep well, HALONAP Customer Care" |
| Honest | Careful claims that we can back up | Hype, "best", "100%" | "Designed to help reduce unwanted light while resting." |
| Everyday premium | Thoughtful design for daily use | Luxury snobbery or exclusivity | "Soft comfort. Thoughtful design." |

**Tone by context:**
- Support: warm, quick and practical. Sign off "Sleep well". Reply within 24 hours.
- Launch: calm excitement ("Good rest is worth waiting for.").
- Errors: plain apology with the fix ("We're really sorry about this…").
- Sales: benefit-led and honest, with no pressure or fake scarcity.
- Social: soft, visual and lifestyle-led (home, break, on the go).
**Terms we own:** "Better sleep, brighter days." · "Made for switching off." · "Shaped around you." · "Made for rest. Made for everyone."
**Never use (and why):** "zero pressure" (not confirmed); "Halo mask" on its own (confusable with competitor MyHalos); "clinically proven" or health claims (no evidence); urgency like "only 2 left!" (not true, and against Amazon listing rules).
