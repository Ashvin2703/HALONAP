For any privacy question or request, email hello@halonap.com with "Privacy" in the subject.

3. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers who handle your information on our behalf and only as we instruct:

  • Stripe: payment processing and fraud prevention. Stripe also acts as an independent controller for some fraud-prevention and legal purposes (see stripe.com/privacy).
  • Supabase: our database and customer accounts, hosted in London, UK.
  • Vercel: website hosting.
  • Our email provider: to send order, dispatch and customer-care emails.
  • Delivery companies (for example Royal Mail): your name, delivery address and, where needed, phone number, so they can deliver your parcel.
  • GitHub: the text, name and rating of approved reviews are included in our website code so search engines can read them.

We may also share information where the law requires it, for example with HMRC, or to protect our rights. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Transfers outside the UK

Some providers (such as Stripe and Vercel) may process information in the United States or other countries. Where that happens, transfers are protected by UK-approved safeguards, such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Order, payment and refund records: 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as required for tax and accounting.
  • Customer messages: 2 years after your enquiry is resolved.
  • Your account and saved bag: until you ask us to delete your account. Order records are then kept as above.
  • Reviews: while they are published, or until you ask us to remove them.
  • Early-access sign-ups: until you unsubscribe or we launch the product you signed up for.

6. Cookies and similar storage

We only use storage that the website needs to work: keeping you signed in, remembering your bag and remembering your cookie choice. These are saved in your browser (local storage). We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If we add them, they will only run after you agree in our cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of your personal information;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to limit how we use it, or object to us using it on the basis of legitimate interests;
  • ask for information you gave us to be sent to you or another organisation in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example to remove a review or stop news emails.

Email hello@halonap.com to use any of these rights. We will reply within one month. Some rights have limits; for example we must keep order records for tax purposes even if you ask us to delete your account.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.

8. Children

Our website is not aimed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make important changes that affect you, we will tell you.
